# 0.1.1 — Capture Vega Foundation

## Goal
Prove the complete architectural loop:

**Galactic Map → Fleet Movement → Hostile Contact → BattleContext → Tactical Scene → Correct Fleet Data**

## Prototype network
- Sol ↔ Vega
- Vega ↔ Carthage
- Vega ↔ Sirius

## Factions
- Dominion / Continuity
- Sovereign Systems

## Initial fleets
**1st Dominion Fleet — Sol**
- 2 × Sentinel
- 1 × Lancer
- 1 × Bastion

**Vega Defense Fleet — Vega**
- 3 × Pathfinder
- 1 × Hammer

## Done when
1. Four systems are visible on the Galactic Map.
2. Selecting a system exposes owner and stationed fleets.
3. A Dominion fleet can move Sol → Vega through the graph.
4. Hostile co-location creates a BattleContext.
5. TacticalBattle loads with the correct system, attacker, defender, and ship states.
6. Placeholder geometry is acceptable.

## Explicitly out of scope
Weapons, heat, component damage, tactical AI, economy, research, diplomacy, narrative, final art, save/load, procedural generation, multiplayer.
